## 3. Why qualifying setups are so rare right now (design, not bugs)

Your config stacks **six** selective layers between a chart and your phone:

```
universe ~2100 → mcap ≥ 1000 Cr → cap to top-800 by mcap
  → panel prefilter: close > ₹100 + green daily + weekly RSI(14) > 60 + weekly MACD hist > 0
  → pattern: BOS(2-12d) + flush ≥6% + SSL hit (−1%/+1.5%) + no close below + green ≥1.8%/0.25 body
  → score ≥ 70 → alert
```

Quantified with the Aug-18 setup as the reference (flush 12.6%, body 0.72, trend 5/5):

| BOS age at reversal | bounce 2.0% | bounce 2.5% | bounce 3.4% (yours) | bounce 5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 days | 72.8 ✅ | 74.8 ✅ | 78.4 ✅ | 84.8 ✅ |
| 6 days | 68.6 ❌ | 70.6 ✅ | 74.2 ✅ | 80.6 ✅ |

| 11 days | 58.2 ❌ | 60.2 ❌ | 63.8 ❌ | 70.2 ✅ |

- A textbook setup reversing **8–12 days after the BOS with an ordinary 2% bounce
  scores 58–66 → silently dropped** at threshold 70. That's most setups.
- The panel prefilter is structurally at odds with the pattern's best trades: a
  deep flush is what earns 20/20 — and exactly what drags weekly RSI/MACD down.
  Your Aug-18 stock passed at **RSI 61.6 vs the >60 cutoff** (control test).
- The intraday workflow scans **only 3 symbols** (SPORTKING, BAJFINANCE, SPR_AUTO)
  — intraday alerts for anything else are impossible by design.
- The help text / README / workflow inputs claimed the threshold was **55** (and
  60 in another place) while `config.py` says **70** — anyone reasoning from the
  docs would expect ~3× more alerts than they get.

## 5. ⚠️ One thing you should verify yourself (data-source health)

On Aug 19, runs **one hour apart** flapped between firing and not firing
(12:00 ❌ → 13:00 ✅ → 14:00 ❌ … 19:00 ✅). With unchanged code and a closed
market, that pattern points to **some runs failing to fetch fresh bars for that
symbol** — Dhan 429 throttling, a token that's no longer valid 24/7 (Dhan access
tokens are short-lived — a static `DHAN_ACCESS_TOKEN` secret goes stale), or the
yfinance fallback being rate-limited on Actions runner IPs.

**How to check in 30 seconds:** open any recent run → job "Live scan + Telegram
alerts" → look for the `scanned N symbols, X signals, E errors` line and the
`error breakdown:` block underneath. A healthy run shows low single-digit to low
double-digit errors out of ~800. If errors ≈ 800 or you see `401/403 invalid
token`, rotate the Dhan token in repo secrets. From now on the Telegram summary
also carries `errors N` so this is visible from your phone.
